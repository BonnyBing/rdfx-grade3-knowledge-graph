## 单元要点（据课文 Let’s talk / Let’s learn / 附录核验）

### Unit 1 Welcome back to school!（书内 2—11）
- 主题：返校、结识新同学、介绍来自哪里
- 核心表达：Welcome! / Where are you from? / I’m from the UK/Canada/USA/China.
- 词汇：boy, girl, new, friend, today, teacher, student, pupil, he, she；国名 China, USA, Canada, UK

### Unit 2 My family（书内 12—21）
- 主题：介绍家庭成员
- 核心表达：Who’s that man/woman? / He’s my father. / She’s my mother. / Is she your mother? Yes, she is. / No, he isn’t.
- 词汇：father, dad, mother, man, woman, brother, sister, grandmother, grandma, grandfather, grandpa, family

### Unit 3 At the zoo（书内 22—31）
- 主题：动物园动物与外形特征
- 核心表达：It’s so tall! / It has a long nose. / It has small eyes and big ears. / Come here, children!
- 词汇：thin, fat, tall, short, long, small, big, giraffe, children, tail, so

### Recycle 1（书内 32—35）
- 综合复习 Unit 1—3 的听说认读

### Unit 4 Where is my car?（书内 36—45）
- 主题：寻找物品、方位介词
- 核心表达：Where is …? / It’s in/on/under … / Is it in your bag? Yes, it is. / No, it isn’t.
- 词汇：on, in, under, chair, desk, cap, ball, car, boat, map, toy, box

### Unit 5 Do you like pears?（书内 46—55）
- 主题：水果喜好
- 核心表达：Do you like …? / Yes, I do. / No, I don’t. / Sorry, I don’t like … / Me, neither. / Let’s buy some fruit.
- 词汇：pear, apple, orange, banana, watermelon, strawberry, grape, buy, fruit

### Unit 6 How many?（书内 56—65）
- 主题：数量询问（11—20）
- 核心表达：How many … do you see/have? / Open it and see!
- 词汇：eleven—twenty, kite, beautiful

### Recycle 2（书内 66—69）
- 综合复习 Unit 4—6
